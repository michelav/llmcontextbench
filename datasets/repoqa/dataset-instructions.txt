When a tool requires a `workspace_id`, use the `Instance ID`.
Do not invent or modify dataset identifiers.

Available RepoQA operations include `list_symbols(workspace_id, path?, kind?)`.
The `path` and `kind` filters are optional. Use `kind="function"` to search
callable code, including class methods. Exact parser-emitted kinds such as
`method` and `class` may be used when known; additional kinds can appear in
`parsed.json`.
